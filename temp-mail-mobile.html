Using Temporary Email on Mobile: iPhone & Android Guide

Published 2026-01-15 · 6 min read · TempMailPro Team

TempMailPro works perfectly on phones — here's the smoothest mobile workflow.

Open in browser

Visit tempmailpro.pages.dev in Safari or Chrome. Tap 'Copy' to put the address on your clipboard.

Paste into the signup form

Switch to the app or site asking for an email, long-press the field, and paste.

Switch back to grab the code

Return to the TempMailPro tab. The verification email usually arrives in under 10 seconds. Tap it to expand and read the code.

Add to home screen

On iOS use 'Add to Home Screen' for a one-tap launcher that feels like a native app.

Battery & data

The site polls about once every 5 seconds — negligible data and battery impact.
