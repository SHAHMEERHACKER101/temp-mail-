Temp Mail vs Burner Email vs Email Alias: Which Should You Use?

Published 2026-01-15 · 6 min read · TempMailPro Team

All three protect your privacy in different ways. Pick the right tool for the job.

Temporary email

Auto-generated, lives a few minutes, can't send, perfect for one-off signups. Example: TempMailPro.

Burner email

A real account you create on Gmail/Proton with a fake name, used for weeks or months, then abandoned. Useful when you need to reply or keep mail history.

Email alias

An address that forwards to your real inbox (Gmail '+' tags, SimpleLogin, AnonAddy, Apple Hide My Email). Best when you DO want the messages, but want to track who leaked the address.

Decision matrix

Need to reply? Use a burner. Need to receive long-term? Use an alias. One-time download or verification code? Use temp mail.

Combining them

Power users layer all three: temp mail for noise, aliases for newsletters, burners for moderate-trust services, and the real inbox for finance and family only.
