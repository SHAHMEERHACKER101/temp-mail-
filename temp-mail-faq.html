Temporary Email FAQ: 25 Questions Answered

Published 2026-01-15 · 6 min read · TempMailPro Team

Quick answers to the most common questions about disposable inboxes.

Is it really free?

Yes — no tiers, no premium, no signup.

Can I send email?

No. TempMailPro is receive-only to prevent abuse.

Can I keep the same address tomorrow?

Browser localStorage keeps it as long as you don't clear site data.

How many addresses can I create?

As many as you like. Click 'New' anytime.

Do you support attachments?

Yes — images, PDFs and most common formats render in the message preview.

Will signups reject the domain?

Most do not. We rotate domains regularly to keep them off blocklists.

Is HTML email rendered?

Yes, with scripts stripped for safety.

Is there an API?

The underlying network is mail.tm — see their docs for programmatic use.

Why do you show ads?

Hosting and bandwidth cost real money. Ads keep the service free; we never put ads inside your inbox.

Where are you based?

TempMailPro is an independent project hosted on Cloudflare Pages.
