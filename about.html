About TempMailPro

Independent privacy tool · Maintained since 2024

TempMailPro is a free temporary email service built by privacy-focused engineers. Our mission is simple: give every internet user an instant, anonymous inbox so they never have to hand over their real email address to untrusted websites.

Why we built it

Every signup is a tiny privacy decision. Newsletters, downloads, app trials and forum registrations all want your email — and most leak, sell or get breached. TempMailPro lets you complete those signups with a real, working address that self-destructs.

How it works

When you open TempMailPro we generate a unique random mailbox on a public relay (powered by the open mail.tm network). Incoming messages appear within seconds. When you close the tab or click "New", the inbox is gone.

Our principles

  • No accounts. We never ask for your name, phone or real email.
  • No tracking inside the inbox. Ads only appear around the tool, never injected into messages.
  • No retention. Messages are stored only as long as the relay keeps them — typically minutes.
  • Open & transparent. Read our Privacy Policy and Terms.
