Privacy Policy

Last updated: January 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how TempMailPro ("we", "us") collects and uses information when you use our website at tempmailpro.pages.dev (the "Service").

1. Information we collect

We do not require registration. We do not collect your name, real email, phone number or address. The Service automatically generates a random temporary mailbox in your browser and stores a small session token in localStorage so the same inbox persists across page reloads.

2. Server logs

Our hosting provider (Cloudflare Pages) may log standard request data (IP address, user agent, timestamp) for security and abuse prevention. These logs are rotated automatically and are not used for advertising profiling.

3. Email content

Messages received in your temporary inbox are stored by the upstream relay (mail.tm) for a short period and then deleted. We do not back up, archive or read your messages.

4. Cookies and advertising

TempMailPro displays advertising provided by Google AdSense. Google and its partners may use cookies to serve ads based on your prior visits to this and other websites. You may opt out of personalised advertising by visiting Google Ads Settings or aboutads.info.

Third-party vendors, including Google, use cookies to serve ads based on a user's prior visits. Google's use of advertising cookies enables it and its partners to serve ads to our users based on their visit to our site and/or other sites on the Internet.

5. Children's privacy

The Service is not directed to children under 13. We do not knowingly collect data from children.

6. GDPR & CCPA

If you are in the EEA, UK or California, you have the right to access, correct or request deletion of any personal data we may hold about you. Because we do not maintain user accounts, in most cases we hold nothing tied to your identity. Contact us via the contact page with any request.

7. Changes

We may update this policy occasionally. Material changes will be reflected on this page with a new "Last updated" date.

8. Contact

Questions about this policy? Reach us through our contact page.
